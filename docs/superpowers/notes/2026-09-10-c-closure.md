# C — the nineteen defects closed, `--probable-top`, and what a stricter margin arm would cost

**Date:** 2026-09-10. **Tree:** branch `rust-core`, from `cce1d66` (V9). **Machine:** Apple M2 Pro,
10 cores (6P + 4E), 16 GB, macOS 24.6.0, rustc 1.97.0, `--release`, backend `cpu`. **What this step
is:** the closing task of the acceptance range — it takes the nineteen defects task V9 left open,
adds one flag a real collection needs, and measures six variants of the tier's margin arm on
evidence that is already on disk. **It changes no decision the tier makes.** The only behaviour that
moves is how many rows of the probable band a report *shows*.

**The verdict in four sentences.** *All nineteen defects are closed* — four of them in the shipped
binary's own text and the gate script, nine in D, six in the two notes and ten in the README
(several defects touch more than one file), and every number that moved was re-derived here rather
than copied. *One of them needed a run rather than an edit*: V9-D2's `--object-disagreement` figure was
measured on this tree instead of being carried from task O1, and it is **27 of 38 confirmed joins
demoted, every one correct**, not "nine of eleven". *`--probable-top N` (default 100) cuts one
ranked list that `report.md`'s Probable section, its per-fragment index and `--review-images` all
read*, so on `mixed_all` a bench gets 100 rows and 123 pictures instead of 2 761 and 2 784, with the
whole band still in `report.json` and byte-identical outputs wherever the band is shorter than N —
which is every development set. *The margin-arm measurement has one clear answer*: **only two of the
six variants reach zero false joins on `mixed_all`**, requiring the support arm alone costs
**124 of 189** correct joins on the acceptance sets, and requiring **agreement between two seeds**
costs **58 of 239** and removes the same ten false joins.

## 2. V9-D5 — the README was corrected and the code was not, and this is why

The brief allowed either: make `different_object` filter pairs before matching, like
`must_not_join`, or correct the README's claim that it already does — the former "if it is a
ten-line change". **It is not a ten-line change, and it would be a worse rule.** Three reasons, in
the order they weigh:

1. **It would weaken the guarantee it exists for.** `different_object` is a statement about
   *objects*, and `assembly/greedy.rs` enforces it as one: before two groups merge, the veto is
   checked for **every member of one against every member of the other**
   (`greedy.rs:402`), so two pots named as different can never end up in one group *by any path*.
   A pre-matching filter removes one pair from the run and leaves every other path between those
   two pots open. On a collection where the conservator names a handful of pairs, that is the
   difference between a guarantee and a hope.
2. **It would destroy the evidence a bench needs.** Today the pair is matched, scored and banded,
   and only the placement is refused: `## Confirmed joins` still holds the row, and `## Confirmed
   joins not used` names it with the reason immediately below. That is how a conservator finds out
   that the geometry disagrees with them — measured on the terracotta in §3, where a
   `different_object` pair comes back with five scored candidates. A filtered pair has no row, no
   score and no picture.
3. **It is not ten lines.** `Resolved::forbids` is one line, but the semantics are stated in
   `Resolved::veto`, `constraints.rs`'s two doc blocks, `## Constraints`'s outcome sentences, the
   `--constraints` help, D §9's flag row, D §12's step-9 row, the README's own constraints table
   and three tests, and `## Confirmed joins not used` would lose a case it is written for.

So the README's museum section now says what the tree does: `different_object` acts **in the
assembly**, it costs the same wall clock as no constraint at all, it takes **pairs of fragments**
rather than pot names (separating Pot_E from Pot_I on `mixed_all` is 34 × 30 = 1 020 lines), and
the list that really does remove a pair before matching — and save its time — is `must_not_join`.

*One nuance left as it is:* the Confirmed section's sentence "The assembly above is built from these
and from nothing else" stays. It says no join outside that table was used, which is true; the
section that follows names the confirmed joins the assembly did not use, and changing the sentence
would move `report.md`'s bytes on every run that has one.

## 3. `--probable-top N`

A real ten-pot collection puts **2 761** pairs in the probable band and 61 of them are true joins;
task A1 §3.2 measured that the first hundred rows, ordered by `seam · tight`, hold **four fifths**
of everything true in it and that one in two of those hundred is real. Until this step `report.md`
printed all 2 761 and `--review-images` drew a PNG for each of the 2 784 confirmed-or-probable
pairs — about 1.8 GB.

`tiers::probable_shown(candidates, top)` ranks the band and cuts it, and returns the length of the
whole band beside the cut. **One list, three consumers**: the Probable section, the per-fragment
index and the review images, so a row, its picture and the count cannot disagree. When the cut bites
the section says so and names where the rest is; `report.json` is never cut.

Tried on `pot_H` (11 fragments, 13 probable pairs), seed 0, `--probable-top 5 --review-images`:

```
## Probable joins
**5 of 13 shown**, best score first (`--probable-top 5`; `0` shows the whole band). The rows below
the cut are in `report.json`, which is never cut, and they have no review image.
```

The five rows are the five highest scores (20.35, 19.26, 16.98, 16.70, 16.08); the per-fragment
index carries the same five pairs and no others, says so in one sentence, and every one of its rows
links a picture; `review/` holds exactly **5** PNGs. `--probable-top 0` prints all thirteen and no
count sentence, which is byte for byte what the run printed before the flag existed.

**Byte identity at the default.** Four sets at seed 0 on the CPU — terracotta, `pot_A`, `pot_H`,
`synthetic_20` — previews and meshes on, `--no-cache`, run once with a binary built at `cce1d66`
and once with this tree's:

| terracotta | 10 | 8 | 2 | **0** | 0 |
| `pot_A` | 16 | 14 | 2 | **0** | 13 |

## 4. The margin arm — six variants, simulated on evidence already recorded

**No tier was changed by this section.** It is a measurement, and it is the input for the step that
decides whether the margin arm stays as it is.

### 4.1 Method

Every variant only ever *restricts* the margin arm, so each variant's confirmed set is a **subset**
of the shipped one and nothing has to be re-probed. The inputs are 46 finished runs already on
disk — the six acceptance runs of `output/acceptance/` (three collections × seeds 0–1) and the forty
development runs of `output/g/sweep/` (eight sets × seeds 0–4), whose `report.json` files reproduce
`output/quality/quality.json` cell for cell (checked here: 40 runs, 0 mismatches on the confirmed
sets and the probable counts). Each confirmed representative is classified by `quality_gate.py`'s
own rule — `evaluate.py` on the candidate's **own** pose, 5° and 0.5 t, `object_of` first — and then
kept or dropped by each rule:

| | rule |
|---|---|
| **(a)** | as shipped: `support ≥ 1` **or** `margin ≥ 2` |
| **(b)** | `support ≥ 1` only — the margin arm removed |
| **(c)** | the margin arm requires a rival R §6.5 **accepted** (the rival is `score / margin`, matched in the pair's own candidate list; **0 unmatched and 0 ambiguous** over all 46 runs) |
| **(d)** | the margin arm requires `margin ≥ 2` **and** `score ≥ 29.18`, the median `seam · tight` of the 130 correct confirmed joins of the development runs (range 6.32–72.39) |
| **(e)** | the margin arm requires the pair to be confirmed at **both** seeds 0 and 1 of that collection |
| **(f)** | the margin arm requires both fragments watertight **and** `seam ≥ 8 t` |

The confirming arms over all 46 runs: **185 margin-only, 104 support-only, 40 both** (329 confirmed
joins, 319 correct and 10 false). Variants (c)–(f) can only touch the 185.

### 4.3 Which variants reach zero false joins on `mixed_all`, and what each costs

| variant | false on `mixed_all` | correct on `mixed_all` | correct, acceptance (189) | correct, development (130) | verdict |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **(a)** shipped | **10** | 41 | 189 | 130 | today |
| **(b)** support only | **0** | 16 (−25) | 65 (**−124**) | 79 (−51) | zero false, and the tier stops finding joins |
| **(c)** accepted rival | 4 | 23 (−18) | 72 (−117) | 83 (−47) | **fails**: keeps all four false joins of seed 0 |
| **(d)** score ≥ median | 1 | 20 (−21) | 71 (−118) | 99 (−31) | **fails** by one, and costs as much as (b) |
| **(e)** two seeds | **0** | 29 (−12) | 145 (−44) | 126 (−4)† | zero false at a quarter of (b)'s cost |
| **(f)** watertight, seam ≥ 8 t | 10 | 41 (−0) | 177 (−12) | 130 (−0) | **inert**: removes nothing |

† (e) over the development sets is quoted under the rule *"confirmed at this seed and at least one
other of the five"*, which is what a five-seed sweep can answer; under the strict seed-0-and-1
reading over the two seeds it keeps 36 of the 50 correct joins those two seeds confirm.

**Two variants reach zero false joins on `mixed_all`: (b) and (e).** What separates them is the
price. (b) removes the margin arm, and the margin arm is what confirms **185 of the 329** joins in
this population: it takes the acceptance sets from 189 correct to 65, `synthetic_170` seed 1 from 48
to 7, and **the terracotta from two joins to none at every seed** — which fails audit §E's step-8
row outright rather than by one slot. (e) keeps the arm and asks the pair to survive a change of
seed: 181 of 239 correct joins over the seed-0/1 runs, all ten false joins gone, and the
terracotta's two joins kept.

**Why (e) works is visible in the ten false joins.** Every one of them is margin-only, every one is
between two watertight fragments, every seam is ≥ 8.3 t — and **not one of the ten is confirmed at
the other seed**. They are draws of R §5.7's search, not properties of the two sherds, which is
exactly what A1 §5 said the seed does to a band. The cost is two runs: 60 minutes instead of 30 on
`mixed_all`, and no code today does it.

**Why the other three fail, precisely.**

* **(c)** is upside down on the collection it matters on. Of the 185 margin-only joins, 170 beat a
  rival R §6.5 refused and 15 beat one it accepted — but on `mixed_all` **seed 0 all four false
  joins beat an accepted rival** (Pot_D 20+26, Pot_E 13+27, Pot_I 03+14, Pot_I 07+08), so (c) keeps
  every one of them while dropping 47 correct joins on the development sets. At seed 1 all six false
  joins beat refused rivals and (c) removes them all. A rule whose answer flips with the seed is not
  a rule.
* **(d)** is one join short. Nine of the ten false joins score below the development median of
  29.18; the tenth, Pot_I 07+08 at **32.21** with a 36.7 t seam, is a wrong-pose join that scores
  better than half the true ones. A threshold on `seam · tight` cannot separate a long clean slide
  along a break from a real join, which is what M1 §3 already measured about `score`.
* **(f)** is inert twice over. Since task G no join with an unwatertight fragment can be confirmed
  at all — **0 of the 329** here have one — so that half of the rule is vacuous; and the ten false
  joins have seams of 8.3 to 36.7 t, so `seam ≥ 8 t` removes none of them while costing 12 correct
  joins on the acceptance sets (`synthetic_170` 51 → 44 and 48 → 44).

### 4.4 What this leaves for the next step

1. **A second seed is the strongest evidence measured so far, and it is not a threshold.** It costs
   a run rather than a recall band, it removed 10 of 10 false joins here, and it kept 76 % of the
   correct ones. If the tier is to gain an arm, this is the one the evidence points at — as a
   *third* band or a flag (`--confirm-across-seeds`), not as a quiet default: two runs is a decision
   a museum makes, not one a tool makes for it.
2. **The measurement is two collections deep.** Ten false joins at one collection and two seeds is a
   thin base for a rule; `mixed_all` is the only collection here that produces a false confirmed
   join at all. Whatever is built should be re-measured when the museum's own acceptance set
   arrives (audit §C.4).
3. **Nothing here rehabilitates the support arm as a sole criterion.** (b)'s zero false joins cost
   two thirds of the tier's recall and the terracotta's whole row; task A1 §3.5 measured the same
   trade on the acceptance sets and refused it, and this step refuses it again with the development
   sets' numbers beside it.
