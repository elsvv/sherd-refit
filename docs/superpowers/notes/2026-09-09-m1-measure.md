# M1: measure before the tiers — every accepted candidate, and the object features

**Date:** 2026-09-09. **Tree:** branch `rust-core`, previous step `364a838` (F7.2).
**Step:** 7 of the audit's amended plan (`notes/2026-09-09-fable-audit.md` §E; D §12 row 7).
**What it is:** the measurement audit §D.1 and §D.2 ask for *before* a confidence tier is written,
and the thresholds chosen on it. No behaviour of `run` changed; two new flags produce two new files
and nothing else.

Everything below is measured on this tree with `target/release/sherd-refit-rs` on the CPU.

## 0. What was built

| where | what |
|---|---|
| `crates/sherd-core/src/measure.rs` | the measurement pass: margin, determinedness, stability under two redraws, the slide probe, the support count, and the JSON they are written as |
| `crates/sherd-core/src/fragment/features.rs` | audit §D.2's per-fragment object features: the outer-shell sphere, the fracture roughness, the axis of rotation and its residual, the rim flag and its diameter, and Lab colour |
| `crates/sherd-core/src/pipeline.rs` | `RunOptions::measure`: one pass after R §8's assembly, before the BVHs are released |
| `crates/sherd-cli/src/main.rs` | `run --measure FILE`, `segment --features FILE [--features-colour]` |
| `tools/measure_tiers.py` | the driver and the two aggregates: the forty runs, `evaluate.py`'s class for every accepted candidate, the AUCs, the threshold search, the feature tables |

Both switches are off by default, and `run` without `--measure` writes the same bytes it wrote
before them (§6).

### The forty runs

`tools/measure_tiers.py --stage runs` is `tools/quality_gate.py`'s forty runs — the same eight sets,
the same seeds 0–4, the same `--backend cpu --no-preview --no-meshes` — with `--measure` added.
**634.8 s (10.6 min)** for the forty, against the gate's own **410.2 s** in the same session: the
measurement pass costs about half a run again. It produced **2 916** accepted-candidate rows, of
which **812** are the best accepted candidate of their pair — the one R §8's `best_per_pair` would
consider, and the unit
every table below is over. Four of those 812 are `unscorable` (pot_C has one fragment without a
ground-truth pose), leaving **808**: **342 correct** and **466 false**.

### How a candidate is classified

`tools/evaluate.py`'s own rule, applied to the candidate's **own** pose rather than to the
assembly's — so a candidate R §8 never used still has a class, which is the whole point of
measuring accepted candidates rather than used joins. `cross_object`, `non_adjacent` and
`wrong_pose` are false joins; `unscorable` is dropped. The terracotta has no staged ground truth,
so R §13's decision row is read literally instead: an accepted candidate on 021–094 or 094–104 is
correct and an accepted candidate on any other pair of that collection is false.

### The probes, and where each one restarts

Two probes re-climb R §5.6's ladder, and both restart from the candidate's **own final pose**: the
stage-1 pose it grew out of is not on the `Candidate` (R §5.7 keeps the pose, the scores and the
verdict), so a probe that wanted it would have to re-run R §5.4 for every pair.

* **`determined`** — the twelve one-ULP neighbours of the pose, climbed through R §5.6's **last two
  rungs**, which is the audit's own wording ("a candidate whose last two rungs move under one-ULP
  perturbations is not confirmed"). The deviation is reported, not thresholded; the rotation is
  `pose_gap::frobenius_deg`, whose floor is zero, and not the trace form, whose floor on these poses
  is 3.6e-2 degrees.
* **`slide`** — ±0.5 t along the seam, then the ladder again. The audit says "the last fracture
  rung"; measured, that rung alone cannot answer, because its correspondence radius is `0.04 t` and
  the push is `0.5 t`: it left the terracotta's two true joins **0.49 t** away, which is the push
  and not a measurement. R §5.6's ladder as a whole starts at `0.2 t` and brings the same two back
  to **0.000 t**. The probe is therefore the whole of R §5.6 restarted, twice per candidate.
  The seam's direction is the principal axis of the breakline points R §6.2 counts as shared — the
  tangent without the per-point sign, which an average of `brk_tangent` would cancel.
* **`stability`** — R §6 again at the same pose on two independent redraws of `Pf`, `S` and the
  margin (`Params::seed` + 1 000 000 and + 2 000 000). The pose is held fixed and only the samples
  move, which is the audit's own form of the probe.
* **`margin`** — the best candidate of the pair that places B more than one wall away
  (`SAME_PLACEMENT_T`, the parity harness's own constant, measured its way: the worst displacement
  over every twentieth vertex of B's working mesh).
* **`support`** — for every third fragment `x` the collection also accepted a join with,
  `T_ax · T_xb` is an independent opinion about where B goes in A's frame; it supports the candidate
  when the two agree inside R §8's tolerances (10°, 0.5 t) **read at B's fracture cloud** and not at
  the file origin, which on these scans is a lever arm of 100–500 units.

Eleven of the twenty-one quantities are above 0.85 and five below 0.65, and the ranking is not the
whole story. The **second AUC column** is the same measurement over the subset of pairs whose kept
list holds more than one placement, and it is what makes the margin readable at all. Six results are
worth stating in words.

**The gap is the best single number there is** (AUC 0.989, 0.998 on the pairs with a second
placement, and 0.978 as a fraction of the pair's own limit). A correct join's median fracture gap
runs 0.0027–0.0151 t with a median of 0.0070; a false one runs 0.0064–0.111 t with a median of
0.0341. R §6.5's own limit is 0.02–0.03 t, five times looser than where the two classes part, and
the two ranges overlap only between 0.0064 and 0.0151 t.

**The margin as audit §D.1 words it is vacuous on two thirds of the rows, and excellent on the
rest.** A pair's kept list (R §5.7's `keep` = 5) holds **one placement** on 270 of the 342 correct
joins and on 245 of the 466 false ones: several stage-1 poses of a pair routinely converge on one
fit, and "the second-best placement of the same pair" then does not exist. Over all rows, reading a
missing rival as an infinite margin gives AUC 0.674; over the rows that *have* a rival, the margin
is **0.921**, the fourth-best quantity measured. That is why the chosen tier reads it strictly — a
missing rival is a failed test, not a passed one — and why it needs a second arm for the rows where
it says nothing.

**Determinedness separates and cannot be thresholded.** AUC 0.879 (0.897 with a rival), the best of
the three new probes, and yet the whole range is 1.6e-14 to 4.1e-7 degrees: every candidate of every
run is determined by any tolerance a person would write down. It is a *ranking*, not a gate, and the
tier does not use it. The same for `determined_t` (0.857, range 3.4e-15 to 3.0e-7 t).

**The slide probe works and costs two ladders** (AUC 0.935, 0.960 with a rival). It is the only
probe that reaches the seam's own degree of freedom: pot_G's used joins, which R §13 already calls
wrong-pose joins on adjacent pairs, sit **1.81 t** and **3.88 t** away after the push at seed 0 and
**8.71 t** at seed 4, where the terracotta's two true joins come back to 1e-14 t. It does not catch
every wrong-pose join, because a slide that is itself a local optimum returns to itself: pot_G's
seeds 1, 2 and 3 come back to 4e-13 t and are still 1.06 t and 2.2° from the truth.

**Two of the audit's own strict thresholds are nearly inert on this material.** `cont_n` has AUC
0.606 over all rows and **0.490** — chance — over the rows with a rival, because a false join that
lays two fracture surfaces together also lays the two shells flush; and `pen` is 0.682 only because
every correct join has `pen` exactly 0 while a handful of false ones reach 0.005. They are kept in
the chosen tier because they cost almost nothing and because R §6.5 already explains them to a
conservator, not because this measurement shows them doing work.

**The support count is the only quantity that separates the hard cases.** Its AUC reads 0.635, which
badly understates it: **every one** of the 466 false joins has a support count of zero, and 92 of the
342 correct ones have one or more. AUC is a ranking statistic and a quantity that is zero on 79 % of
the positives cannot rank well; what it is, is a one-sided certificate. It is what audit §D.2 calls
cycle consistency, and the plan puts it in step 10.

## 3. The candidate tiers, and the one chosen

`correct` and `false` are over all forty runs, on the same rows as the table above. **Worst
rejection** is the tier read as the conjunction it is: for every false join, the largest *relative*
distance by which some active test throws it out, and then the smallest of those over the false
joins. A tier that reaches zero false joins by sitting 0.5 % outside one boundary is fitted to these
forty runs; one whose worst false join is a fifth outside something has a threshold. `inf` means the
worst false join is thrown out by a probe that could not answer for it at all.

### The chosen thresholds

```
confirmed  =  tight   >= 0.35
           AND gap     <= 0.015 t          (R §6.5 ships 0.02-0.03 t)
           AND seam    >= 5 t
           AND cont_n  >= 0.90
           AND pen     <= 0
           AND slide   <= 0.1 t            (audit §D.1's own number)
           AND ( support >= 1  OR  margin >= 2 over a second placement that scores )
```

**136 confirmed correct joins, 0 false, over the eight development sets at seeds 0–4.** That is
39.8 % of the 342 correct joins R §6.5 accepts and **17.7 % of the 770 ground-truth adjacent pairs**
the forty runs could have found. Seven of the eight sets contribute; pot_G contributes nothing
because it has no correct join to confirm at any seed.

**The false-join margin.** Read as the conjunction it is, the worst false join over all forty runs is
thrown out by **37 %** of a boundary: `mixed_ABG` seed 1, `Pot_A_Piece_08`–`Pot_B_Piece_07`, a
cross-object join whose gap is 0.0206 t against the limit of 0.015 t. Every other false join is
further out. That is a threshold; the numbers below are what a fit looks like.

**Why not the search's own answer.** The search's best zero-false conjunction is `gap ≤ 0.0064 t`
and nothing else, and it confirms **141** — five more. Its nearest false join sits **3.3e-5 t**
outside the boundary, 0.5 % of it: the threshold is a fit to these forty runs and one more seed
would put a false join inside it. Loosening it until the margin is real costs the recall it bought —
`gap ≤ 0.006 t` confirms 120 at a 7 % margin, `gap ≤ 0.005 t` confirms 67 at 29 % — and at 0.005 t
**63 of the 67** are `synthetic_20`, which is a threshold that has learned the synthetic set rather
than the material.

**Why the margin is read strictly.** `m` = 2 is audit §D.1's own proposal and the measurement leaves
it there: 1.5 and 2 confirm the same 136 joins, 3 confirms 132, 4 confirms 130 and 5 confirms 128,
so 2 sits on a plateau rather than on a cliff. What the measurement *does* change is the reading of
a missing rival: a pair whose kept list holds one placement fails the margin test rather than
passing it with an infinite margin. Read the other way round the test is not merely weaker, it is
**inert**: `strict ∧ gap ≤ 0.015 t ∧ slide ≤ 0.1 t` confirms 293 correct and 18 false, and adding a
loose `margin ≥ 2` to it removes neither a correct join nor a false one.

**Why the support count is in a tier the plan put it after.** Without it, the highest-recall
zero-false rule is the fitted `gap ≤ 0.0064 t`; with it, the pot_H and pot_A wrong-pose family is
refused by evidence rather than by a threshold sitting 0.5 % away from it. Audit §D.2 puts the
support count in step 10 and §D.1 does not mention it. **It has to move into step 8**, or step 8
cannot state "zero false joins" at all. It is cheap: a walk over the accepted-candidate graph,
no geometry.

**What the tier cannot do, and what step 8's gate must say instead.** Audit §E's step-8 gate reads

### The verdict on the features

**No feature earns a veto.** On the three collections whose object ids are real — `mixed_ABG` (the
development test), `mixed_all` (the acceptance set) and the pooled `pots_A_H` — the best AUC of any
feature is **0.740** (`shell_radius` on `mixed_ABG`), then 0.695 and 0.694 (`thick_mode` and `thick`
on `pots_A_H`) and 0.663 (`thick` on `mixed_all`). Audit §D.2's own rule is 0.800, so **every one of
them reports and none of them vetoes**. The only two figures above 0.8 are `thick_mode` 0.845 and
`thick` 0.798 on `synthetic_170`, whose "different object" is six intruder fragments of a second
generated vessel with a deliberately different wall — a split the generator made, not a measurement
of ceramics.

**A `k·MAD` veto is far worse than the ratio gate scale-pairs §4.3 measured.** At k = 2 the best
feature on `mixed_all` (`thick`) removes 35 % of the pairs and 7 % of the adjacent ones; on
`mixed_ABG` it removes 48 % of the pairs and **43 %** of the adjacent ones, because `mixed_ABG`'s
three pots are the twins scale-pairs already named (A, B and G differ by 0.7 units of wall). MAD is
the wrong scale for a quantity whose within-object spread is a fifth of its between-object spread.

**The ratio gate does not reproduce §4.3's numbers with this shell radius.** §4.3 measured "2× on
thickness and 3× on radius keeps 66 % of `mixed_all`'s pairs and loses 6 % of its adjacent ones";
with the sphere fit here it keeps **54.4 %** and loses **15.6 %**. The two radii are different
quantities — §4.3's is a curvature radius, this one is a global sphere through the outer-shell
samples — and the medians say so: pot A reads 109 here and 23 there, pot H 69 against 48. On
`mixed_ABG` the same gate keeps 96 % of the pairs and **all 40** adjacent ones, which is the honest
statement of what it is worth on three pots.

**The rim flag never fires.** R §3.2's `thick_mode > 1.15 · thick` is set on **2 of 517** fragments
across every collection measured (both in `synthetic_170`), so `rim_diameter` has two values in the
whole benchmark and audit §D.2's rim-diameter spike has nothing to be calibrated on. The axis fit
itself is sound — its residual is 0.012–0.080 on the pots, and a synthetic cylinder gives 0 — but
the diameter it produces (`axis_diameter`) separates nothing: AUC 0.528 on `mixed_ABG`, 0.537 on
`mixed_all`, 0.411 on `synthetic_170`.

**Colour is unmeasurable on every set that has object ids, exactly as audit §C.4 predicted.**
Measured rather than assumed: the SfS++ OBJs of pots C–H carry **one** RGB triple each (a flat
L = 77.7, a = b = 0 grey), pot A and pot B carry none at all, `mixed_all` has 147 of 164 fragments
with that same flat colour, and both synthetic sets carry a near-constant terracotta whose Lab AUC
on `synthetic_170`'s main-vs-intruder split is **0.447**. The only real vertex colour in the
benchmark is the museum's four terracotta scans — 24 000–32 000 distinct triples each, Lab mean
(57.9, 12.3, 20.7), spread 5.6 in L — and they are one object, so they yield no AUC either. The
feature is implemented and correct (`fragment::features::srgb_to_lab`, unit-tested against the
standard's white point); it has nothing to be calibrated on until the museum's own scans arrive.

**What the shortlist is.** Empty, under §D.2's own rule. `shell_radius` (0.740 on `mixed_ABG`) and
`thick` (0.663 on `mixed_all`) are the two worth carrying into step 10's `GroupFeatures` as
*reported* consensus numbers — the conservator sees them beside the review image — and neither may
demote a join on this evidence.

## 5. What step 8 and step 10 should do with this

1. **Move audit §D.2's support count into step 8.** Without it there is no zero-false tier that is
   not a fit; with it there is one, at 136 confirmed joins over the forty runs.
2. **Ship the chosen thresholds of §3** and record them in `Params` with their off switch, as the
   step's brief requires.
3. **Read the margin strictly**: no second placement is a failed margin test, not an infinite one.
4. **Drop determinedness from the tier** and keep it as a reported number. Its AUC is 0.879 and its
   whole range is 1.6e-14 to 4.1e-7 degrees; there is no threshold in it.
5. **Keep the slide probe** at 0.1 t, restarted on the whole of R §5.6 and not on its last rung.
   It costs two ladders per accepted candidate and it is the only probe that reads the seam's own
   degree of freedom.
6. **Restate step 8's terracotta gate** as four of five seeds confirmed and the fifth probable, or
   raise `keep` so that the margin arm always has a rival (an algorithm change, not proposed here).
7. **Let no object feature veto in step 10.** Report `shell_radius` and `thick` per group; the
   measured AUC of every feature on every collection with real object ids is below 0.75.
8. **The three redraws cost more than they buy here** (136 → 130 confirmed, no false join removed),
   and they are kept out of the chosen tier. They stay worth their two verifications as a *reported*
   stability number, and they are the honest answer to "would another seed have found this join".
